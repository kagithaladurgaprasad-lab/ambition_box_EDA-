# 🚀 Corporate Employee Trends & Compensation Analysis

An end-to-end **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** project that analyzes corporate hiring trends, employee compensation, company reviews, interview experiences, and workforce distribution using real-world data collected through web scraping.

This project transforms raw employment data into meaningful business insights, helping understand hiring patterns, salary trends, employee satisfaction, and company performance across industries.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Understanding employee compensation, hiring trends, and workplace satisfaction has become increasingly important for organizations and job seekers alike.

In this project, I collected and analyzed real-world corporate data from an online employment platform using web scraping techniques. Through comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), I uncovered valuable insights into salary distribution, hiring patterns, company reviews, interview experiences, and workforce trends.

The project demonstrates how data-driven analysis can support informed business decisions and improve talent acquisition strategies.

---

# 🔍 What I Did

- Collected corporate employee data using automated **Web Scraping**.
- Cleaned and preprocessed the dataset by handling missing values, removing duplicates, and standardizing salary information.
- Performed **Univariate, Bivariate, and Multivariate Analysis**.
- Analyzed hiring trends across leading companies.
- Explored employee compensation patterns across industries.
- Studied relationships between salaries, employee ratings, and interview experiences.
- Created interactive and insightful visualizations using Python libraries.
- Derived business recommendations based on analytical findings.

# 📈 Key Insights

### 📌 Hiring Trends

- Hiring opportunities are concentrated among a few dominant organizations.
- **Accenture** emerged as one of the largest recruiters.

### 💰 Salary Analysis

- Salary distribution is highly skewed across companies.
- Companies such as **TCS** appear as significant compensation outliers.

### ⭐ Employee Satisfaction

- Higher salaries generally correspond to better employee reviews.
- Competitive compensation plays a significant role in employee satisfaction.

### 🏙 Location Analysis

- **Bengaluru** and **Hyderabad** dominate hiring opportunities.
- Major metropolitan cities consistently offer higher salary packages and more employment opportunities.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights that competitive compensation is one of the strongest factors influencing employee satisfaction and organizational reputation.

Organizations aiming to attract and retain top talent should align their salary structures with market standards while maintaining a positive work environment.

These insights can assist HR teams, recruiters, and business leaders in making informed hiring and compensation decisions.
